Summary

Moon Miner is set on Vanth VI, a scarred lunar mining moon. You play a stranded maintenance worker who has to survive, explore, mine resources, build a base and fight off alien threats in order to repair a damaged ship and escape.

It is a Unity and C# group project inspired by Terraria and Minecraft, taken through a full development cycle from pre-production to a playable MVP.

Aims

Design a complete core gameplay loop of exploring, mining, building and surviving, and deliver it as a stable, playable build. The loop is held together by progression: better gear opens up harder areas and tougher enemies.

Methods

The team worked through design before code, then built and tested in iterations. Task tracking lived in Trello and all code in Git.

  • Mining: tools with durability and weak points to hit
  • Base building: structural integrity and environmental hazards
  • Suit upgrades: active and passive abilities
  • Enemies: procedural encounters
  • Documentation: context and use-case diagrams, system architecture, requirements analysis, SWOT and PEST analyses, and a budget breakdown
Results

The team delivered a playable MVP alongside a full set of design documentation. My contribution covered gameplay programming, the retro sci-fi art direction and the testing that kept features stable between iterations.
