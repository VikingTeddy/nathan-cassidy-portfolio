The concept

Datacentres power AI, but most people never see inside one. This project lets players step in and explore it for themselves.

The idea is an interactive experience rather than a lecture. Instead of reading about racks, cooling and power, the player moves through a real-feeling space, discovers areas by opening doors, and learns by looking around.

Design pillars

Explore
First-person movement lets players wander the facility at their own pace, with no fixed route.
02
Discover
Doors and interactions reveal new areas, so progress comes from curiosity.
03
Believe
Materials and lighting are tuned in Unity so the space reads as real hardware, not a grey box.
